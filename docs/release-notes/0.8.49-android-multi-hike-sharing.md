# HikeJournal Android 0.8.49 — Multi-hike day sharing (build 187)

## Release scope

Android only. The hike share flow can now combine outings recorded on the same
date into one social carousel. Canonical `VERSION` and Android `versionName`
remain **0.8.49**; Android `versionCode` advances from **186 to 187**.

## Android

- From a hike's share preview, choose **Combine outings from this day** to add
  other hikes recorded on the same date.
- Select photos separately for each included hike. Hike details and photos are
  loaded when an outing is added; route data is used in its keepsake preview
  when available.
- Choose keepsake cards first, last, or omit them. When included, every selected
  hike contributes its own card, and all cards appear together before or after
  the selected photos.
- The combined share remains within the 20-image carousel limit. Each keepsake
  card uses one slot; videos are excluded. The share caption lists every hike,
  its date/time, and distance when available.
- Existing single-hike sharing and its card placement controls remain available.
- Android metadata: `versionName` **0.8.49**, `versionCode` **187**.
