# HikeJournal Android 0.8.45 — Species quick jump

## Release scope

Android only. The canonical `VERSION` and Android `versionName` remain
`0.8.45`; Android `versionCode` advances from `177` to `178`.

## Android

- Show a right-edge quick-jump rail when the filtered Collection contains at
  least 30 species and the species rows are in view. Short lists keep the
  existing layout.
- Let people tap or drag along the rail. Common-name and scientific-name sorts
  jump to the first species under each visible initial; encounter and recent
  sorts offer five evenly spaced list positions.
- Keep jump targets aligned with the active search, outing and life filters,
  and reserve space at the end of species rows so the rail does not cover their
  content. The rail also exposes jump actions to accessibility services.
- Android metadata: `versionName` `0.8.45`, `versionCode` `178`.
