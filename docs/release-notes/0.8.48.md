# HikeJournal v0.8.48

**Release scope:** Android and iOS. The GitHub release distributes the verified,
signed Android APK. iOS source is built and tested locally; no App Store Connect
or TestFlight upload is performed.

## Identification safety acknowledgment

- New and existing users see an identification-safety notice before entering
  the app. Continuing requires an explicit acknowledgment; simply displaying
  the notice does not accept it.
- State plainly that HikeJournal is independent, is not an official iNaturalist
  app/channel, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by iNaturalist.
- Explain that automated suggestions, community observations, confidence
  scores, and saved/confirmed labels can be wrong and do not guarantee safety.
- Ask users to verify identifications independently with a qualified local
  expert and reliable field references.
- Warn never to eat, taste, prepare food/drink from, or use a wild organism
  based on a HikeJournal ID. App IDs must not determine edibility, toxicity,
  medicinal use, or safe touching/handling.
- Save acknowledgment locally for the current notice version. Ordinary app
  updates do not repeat it; a material notice revision can require a fresh
  acknowledgment. No acknowledgment record is sent to the server.
- Keep the full notice available in Settings, and show short safety reminders
  in photo identification/review and Field Guide species-detail screens.
- Keep Android's direct-photo review dialog scrollable with the added warning.
