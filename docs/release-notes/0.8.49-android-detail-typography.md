# HikeJournal 0.8.49 — calmer detail pages and search

Scope: **Android only**.

## Android

- Move the complete “IDs may be wrong” reminder to the bottom of species
  details, after the final field record.
- Reduce the species hero title to 28 sp and the main name to 30 sp, using
  regular weight. Bring the scientific name, status, summaries, seasonal
  heading, and Field records heading into a quieter hierarchy.
- Reduce encounter hike titles from 20 sp semibold to 16 sp regular, with
  smaller supporting date/location/caption/action text. Preserve the existing
  photo-opening behavior.
- Remove magnifying-glass adornments from all Android search fields: the
  Field Guide, outing selector, known-species assignment, saved places, and
  river-gauge place search. Restore the Field Guide field’s full width; retain
  labels, search behavior, and existing margins.
- Reduce Hikes list/Everyday Sightings titles from 20 sp to 17 sp and use a real
  weight-350 Quicksand instance, slightly lighter than regular. Reduce the
  featured hike title from 44 sp to 36 sp. Other screen typography is unchanged.
- Preserve font attribution/license and the existing HikeJournal Rounded
  derivative family naming. Add only the Android weight-350 face.
- Version name remains **0.8.49**. Increment Android `versionCode` from
  **197** to **198**. Shared `VERSION` is unchanged.
- Platform-qualified tag: `android-v0.8.49-detail-typography`.
