# HikeJournal v0.8.47

**Release scope:** Android and iOS. The GitHub release distributes the signed
Android APK. iOS source and release metadata are included; no App Store Connect
upload is performed by this release.

## Hike sharing on Android and iOS

- Choose **Keepsake first**, **Keepsake last**, or **No keepsake** each time a
  hike is shared. Keepsake first remains the default.
- Keepsake first/last allows up to 19 album photos plus the route-and-distance
  keepsake. No keepsake allows up to 20 album photos.
- The photo limit, Select all action, selection count, and Share button update
  with the selected option. Returning from 20 photos to a keepsake retains the
  first 19 selected photos in their existing order.
- The share preview is hidden when no keepsake is selected, and the export
  skips keepsake generation. The outgoing image list preserves photo order and
  places the keepsake at the requested end.
- Sharing without a keepsake requires at least one selected photo. If every
  selected photo is unavailable, show a retryable error before opening the
  system share sheet. Partially unavailable photos retain the existing omission
  notice and do not change keepsake placement.
- Videos remain excluded from this image-sharing flow. Android's GPS-safe
  original-photo browser and media permissions are unchanged.
