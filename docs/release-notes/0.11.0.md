# HikeJournal 0.11.0 · Clearer edibility and traditional uses

Release scope is Android and API. Android build 207. GitHub tag `v0.11.0`.

## Android

- The Field Guide and optional photo indicators use direct, plant-part labels such as **Berries · Edible**, **Leaves · Toxic**, and **Fruit · Not edible**. A part can also show **Edible with conditions** or **Caution** where the source supports it. The photo reference opens under the species name.
- **Used as food** identifies a checklist entry without a named edible part. When such an entry accompanies a documented hazard, the hazard leads and the broad food record appears as a short context note. Historical poison use appears under **Sources & notes** with its evidence limits.
- **Edibility unknown**, **Species ID needed**, and **Reference needs a refresh** explain distinct reasons an entry lacks a usable answer. The detail section applies to plants and keeps evidence attached to exact species and varieties.
- **Traditional uses** shows cited medicinal and cultural history separately from food and toxicity. The photo and species pages remove repeated safety paragraphs. First-use acknowledgment and the notice in Settings continue to cover identification and use.
- Android `versionName` advances from **0.10.0** to **0.11.0**. Android `versionCode` advances from **206** to **207**.

## API and reference data

- Added 14 cited findings, including 10 historical-use records. The reviewed catalog now contains 57 plant-part findings and 10 traditional-use records across 48 exact species from 44 sources. The new records include historical passionflower use, wax myrtle in candle making, and documented toxicity of yellow jessamine plants and coral bean seeds.
- Added optional `traditional_uses` with claim-level sources and original citations where available. Medicinal and cultural records are independent of edibility and toxicity classification.
- Separated documented toxicity from a reported use as a poison. Existing WCFP profiles are interpreted with that distinction when read. Backfill output now counts poison-use listings separately. The release uses the existing database schema and feature flag.
- The expanded catalog covers 46 entries in the 417-entry Field Guide audit, including 41 with part-level evidence and nine with traditional-use records. USDA's archive, Kew's checklist, and MedWEP provide additional candidates for review. First-time checklist matching and periodic revalidation still require manual backfill. See the [source and coverage audit](../EDIBILITY_REFERENCE.md).
- The shared API version advances from **0.10.0** to **0.11.0**.
