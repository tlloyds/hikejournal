# HikeJournal Android 0.8.43 — Route replay framing and speed

## Release scope

Android only. Route replay now frames the full route above its on-screen
controls and offers `0.5×`, `1×`, and `2×` playback speeds. `VERSION` and
Android `versionName` remain `0.8.43`; Android `versionCode` increases from
`171` to `172`.

## Android

- Fit replay routes into the map area left clear by the header and playback
  controls, so long routes remain visible behind the controls.
- Add accessible `0.5×`, `1×`, and `2×` speed selection. Changing speed while
  playing continues from the current route position.
- Update the displayed replay duration to match the selected speed: 48, 24, or
  12 seconds.
