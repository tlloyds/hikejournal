# HikeJournal v0.8.49

**Release scope:** Android and iOS. This release distributes the verified,
signed Android APK. iOS source is built, tested, and archived locally; no App
Store Connect or TestFlight upload is performed.

## Easier-to-read identification safety notice

- Use larger, heavier body text and a clean, high-contrast background on the
  identification-safety acknowledgment and its Settings copy.
- Select the bundled fonts' actual weight variations, correcting the very
  light default faces. Strengthen headings and emphasize the consumption
  warning.
- Increase paragraph spacing, limit reading width on larger screens, and
  underline the iNaturalist guidance link.
- Keep the acknowledgment button label readable while disabled and allow it
  to wrap at accessibility text sizes.
- Make short identification reminders larger, heavier, and fully opaque,
  including reminders over the dark photo-review background.
- Support iOS light/dark appearance, Dynamic Type, and Bold Text. Android
  retains its established light appearance and supports enlarged text.
- Preserve the notice wording and saved acknowledgment for notice version 1.

Measured body-text contrast is at least **16.35:1** on the light notice and
**14.86:1** on the iOS dark notice. The guidance link and button labels also
exceed 7:1 in their defined palettes. These are color-contrast measurements,
not a claim of complete app accessibility conformance.
