# HikeJournal Android 0.8.49 — Design alignment (build 194)

**Release scope: Android only.** This release aligns the native app with the
corrected Hike Journal artwork and the supplied Hikes, Field Guide, Trail
Medals, Species Review, and Publish references.

## Android changes

### Brand and typography

- Replaced the old stacked “Hiking Journal” wordmark with a single-line
  **HikeJournal** wordmark, including the Hikes header, species records,
  journal covers, and Place Profiles opened from Plan a Visit.
- Standardized page marks and the launcher on the supplied green outline,
  forest fill, and white interior bars. Removed the obsolete runtime white
  icon and old wordmark image.
- Matched the Illustrator source's Quicksand family, with actual regular,
  medium, semibold, and bold font instances. Corrected the editorial serif
  weights as well. Previously, all weights selected the same variable font's
  light default, making bold titles and labels appear thin.
- Completed the brand palette for native dialogs and filter chips, removing
  inherited purple Material defaults. Small header titles adapt to available
  width, including a 320 dp phone layout.
- Scoped dark navigation-bar icons to Android 8.1+ and kept a contrasting
  forest navigation bar on Android 8.0, resolving an existing lint error.
- Rendered the supplied contour artwork at the design's larger scale and
  removed the extra fading layers that made the pattern nearly invisible.
  Shared headers use white, bold, consistently capitalized titles.

### Hikes and navigation

- Kept HikeJournal on one line beside refresh and settings.
- Removed the separate magnifying glass and gave the search field the full
  content width.
- Matched the featured outing date to the orange add button and made its text
  bold, with dark forest lettering for readability.
- Rebalanced the floating Hikes navigation to a 64 dp capsule and matching
  circular add button, with more deliberate icon, label, and interior spacing.
- Replaced navigation PNGs with exact paths from the supplied SVG icons, so
  they remain crisp at Android screen densities. Enlarged the plus within its
  circle and gave labels real semibold weights.
- Retained the full-width navigation treatment on other main screens and
  extended its background through the Android gesture inset.

### Field Guide and planning

- Used the green outline mark and bold **Field Guide** title over the shared
  contour texture.
- Matched Collection, Nearby, and Field Quests to the reference's full-width
  fern/pale-green tab strip, with bold labels and a flat active state.
- Updated species detail and planning wordmarks and made the Place Profile
  position line bold.

### Trail Medals

- Applied the shared patterned background and green outline header mark.
- Removed “x of y earned · Lifetime progress” from the header.
- Made **NEXT MEDAL** and the progress sentence bold; matched the progress
  bar to fern on a pale track.
- Removed the detail sheet's Close link. Native swipe-down, scrim tap, and
  Back dismissal remain available.
- Aligned detail sheets with the white surface, orange bold medal title, and
  green progress treatment in the reference.

### Species Review

- Standardized the **Species Review** title, logo, texture, and header height.
  Moved Batch ID into the header row between the title and refresh.
- Changed the waiting prompt to **“Not sure what you saw?”** and the primary
  action to **“Identify this sighting”**, with a fern button and forest text.
- Placed Remove from review before the quieter Skip for now action.
- Moved the existing identification warning to an **IMPORTANT:** footer
  beneath the actions, preserving its full safety wording.
- Reserved space above the bottom bar so scrolling cannot place actions or
  the footer under navigation. Sync, offline, connection, confirmation,
  rejection, and batch eligibility rules are preserved.

### Publish

- Applied the shared logo, typography, texture, header height, and full-width
  Ready / Attention / Posted tabs.
- Moved batch posting into the header action area and aligned the outing
  title, bold public-posting prompt, green **Publish now** button, quieter Next
  record action, and safety footer with the supplied design.
- Kept the explicit public-posting confirmation and observation options.
  Reserved bottom navigation space so the publishing action remains tappable.

### Sightings Map

- Removed the logo, used the compact **Sightings Map** title, and condensed
  controls and attribution to give the imagery more room.
- Added top/bottom camera clearance for the header and navigation.
- Forwarded the host lifecycle to MapLibre's native view for background and
  foreground transitions.
- A failed thumbnail now falls back to the original photo. Selected photo
  previews adopt newly refreshed URLs when the viewport returns that record.
- Retained satellite/trail switching, photo markers, routes, trail overlays,
  map display controls, offline packs, photo opening, and journal handoffs.

### Welcome / discovery celebrations

- Replaced the rigid full-screen green layout with a compact, centered photo
  dialog that sizes to its content and available height.
- Reduced the title scale and excess space before the action. Kept the photo
  entrance and content reveal, scrollable long copy, and inset-safe action.
- Navigation selection, section-tab transitions, review record changes,
  celebration reveals, and medal progress retain restrained motion.

The GPS-safe phone-original photo browser, EXIF/GPS handling, media permissions,
and iNaturalist publishing workflow are preserved.
